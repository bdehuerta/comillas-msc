What we do

Real brand challenges, not case studies

01

Campaign Development

You build a marketing campaign from brief to execution: positioning, messaging, channels, timeline. You defend the choices you make and adjust when they don't work.

02

Market Research

You investigate a real market question — a segment, a competitor, a shift in behaviour — using proper research methods, then turn the findings into a recommendation someone could act on.

03

Brand Partnerships

You help scope and manage relationships with outside brands, from the first pitch to the final deliverable, learning what it takes to represent a club professionally.

04

Competitions

You join a team, pick a competition, and go head-to-head with other universities on a live marketing or strategy case, under a deadline, with a result at the end.

The flagship programme

Market Analysts — learn it by running it

Market Analysts is our core programme. Small teams take a real brand challenge through a full cycle — research, strategy, execution, review. No lectures about marketing. You do the marketing, then break down what worked.

How it runs

  • Small teams, one brief each
  • Weekly working sessions, not talks
  • A mentor or lead checks progress
  • Ends in a live presentation

What you take away

  • A finished campaign or research piece
  • Direct feedback on your thinking
  • Experience defending decisions
  • A portfolio piece, not a certificate

Open to every degree at Comillas. No prior marketing coursework assumed — the interesting work happens where people who read markets meet people who read spreadsheets.
