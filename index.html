About

Three things we actually do

Live briefs

We take on real problems from real companies — a launch, a repositioning, a market entry — and work them in small teams over a few weeks. You present to the people who own the decision.

Case & competition prep

Structured practice for case competitions and consulting interviews. Frameworks are the easy part; we drill the reasoning, the numbers and the delivery until they hold up under questions.

Practitioners in the room

Brand leads, founders and strategists who come to be interrogated, not to present slides. Small sessions, unfiltered questions, and the alumni network that comes with them.

Open to every degree at Comillas. Marketing rewards people who can read a market and people who can read a spreadsheet, and the interesting work happens where those two meet.
