Más que un club.

We are the Marketing & Strategy Club at ICADE. We take real briefs from real brands, run workshops and competitions, and train members through our own Market Analysts programme. Every degree welcome.

What you actually do here

01

Campaign Development

Positioning, messaging, channels, timeline. You present the plan and defend it. Then you find out which parts survive contact with a real audience.

02

Market Research

Surveys, interviews, competitor teardowns. The hard part is turning what you found into a recommendation a brand can act on by Monday. That is the part we practise.

03

Brand Partnerships

You sit in on the conversations with the brands we work with, from the first email to the final handover. Some of it is unglamorous. All of it is the job.

04

Competitions

Pick a case competition, join a team, go up against other universities under a deadline. You either place or you learn why you did not.

The Market Analysts programme

Small teams take one real brand challenge through research, strategy, execution and review. You will present to the brand at the end. We meet weekly and the sessions are working sessions.

How it runs

  • Small teams, one brief each
  • Weekly working sessions
  • A mentor or lead checks progress
  • Ends in a live presentation

What you take away

  • A finished campaign or research piece
  • People telling you where your logic breaks
  • Experience defending decisions
  • Something real to show for it

No prior marketing coursework assumed. We take people from every degree at Comillas, and the mix is deliberate.
