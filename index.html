What we do

Four ways members build the craft

01

Campaign development

Teams build campaigns end to end — audience, positioning, creative, channel plan and the measurement that proves it worked. Real constraints, real budgets, real deadlines.

02

Market research

Survey design, interviews, competitive teardowns and the analysis that turns raw responses into a recommendation somebody can act on.

03

Brand partnerships

We work directly with companies on live briefs. Members present to the people who own the decision and leave with work worth putting in a portfolio.

04

Competitions

Structured preparation for national and international marketing and case competitions — the reasoning, the numbers and the delivery, drilled until they hold up under questioning.

Flagship program

The Campaign Sprint

Our core program. A partner company brings a real problem, teams take it on for PLACEHOLDER weeks, and the term ends with presentations to the client and a panel.

How a sprint runs

  • Teams of PLACEHOLDER, mixed by degree and year on purpose
  • A weekly working session with the board, not a lecture
  • One checkpoint with the client at the halfway mark
  • Final presentation to the client and an external panel

What you take away

  • A finished piece of work with a named company attached
  • Direct feedback from practitioners in the room
  • Presentation reps in front of people who ask hard questions
  • The alumni and partner network that comes with it

Open to every degree at Comillas. No prior marketing coursework is assumed — the interesting work happens where people who read markets meet people who read spreadsheets.
